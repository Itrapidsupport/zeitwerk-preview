01/The challenge

No single source of truth.

Portfolio data sat in separate systems and spreadsheets. Every report the leadership team saw had been built by hand, so the numbers were already days out of date by the time anyone acted on them.

At that scale, manual assembly took roughly a full workweek of staff time every week. Simple questions, such as how a property is performing or where the problems are concentrated, took days to answer.

02/What we built

The whole portfolio in one live view.

Zeitwerk designed and built a real-time executive dashboard. Portfolio KPIs calculate automatically, every property sits on a map, and any number on screen can be drilled into, from portfolio level down to a single building.

  • Real-time visibility across all 17,968 units, with no waiting for the weekly report
  • Geospatial mapping with drill-down analytics for every property
  • Automated portfolio KPIs in place of hand-built spreadsheets
Illustrative schematic
03/The result

Manual portfolio reporting, gone.

The weekly assembly exercise became a self-service dashboard. Leadership checks the numbers when they need them, and the numbers are always current. The change saves an estimated 40 hours of staff time a week, roughly $80K a year.
