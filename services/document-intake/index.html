01/The problem

Someone is still typing numbers off a PDF.

Someone is still typing numbers off a PDF.

Invoices, receipts, applications and inspection forms, each one re-keyed by hand. Each one is a chance for a typo, and the minutes add up to someone's entire job.

The re-keying tax

  1. 01Staff spend hours a week copying data from documents into software.
  2. 02Typos and missed fields surface weeks later as accounting or compliance problems.
  3. 03Documents arrive by email, portal and paper, and pile up in all three.
  4. 04Backlogs grow every time volume spikes or someone is away.
  5. 05The people doing the re-keying were hired to do something more useful.

None of this is a staffing problem. It is a workflow that was never designed to scale, and that is what we fix.

02/How it works

How it works
  1. 01

    Inventory your documents

  2. 02

Frequently asked questions
What kinds of documents can it handle?

Invoices, receipts, forms, applications, inspection reports and most other business documents, as native PDFs, scans or phone photos. If a person can read it, it is usually a candidate.

How accurate is the extraction?

Very accurate on well-structured documents, and we do not rely on trust alone. Validation rules and confidence thresholds send anything questionable to a person. In one project listed on our projects page, receipt extraction reached 95%+ accuracy with automatic G/L coding.

Where does the extracted data go?

Wherever it needs to: accounting software, ERP, a database or a spreadsheet. We build the integration so data lands in the system your team already uses.

What happens to the original documents?

They are filed automatically, named consistently and stored where you want them, so the source is always one click from the record it produced.
