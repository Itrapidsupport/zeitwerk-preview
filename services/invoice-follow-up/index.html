01/The problem

Unpaid usually means busy, not unwilling.

Unpaid usually means busy, not unwilling.

Every week an invoice sits unpaid, cash flow tightens and the follow-up call gets more awkward. So it gets postponed, and the problem compounds.

The cost of not chasing

  1. 01Receivables age because follow-up depends on someone remembering.
  2. 02Every customer gets the same generic reminder, whatever their history.
  3. 03Staff dread collection calls, so they happen late or not at all.
  4. 04Nobody can say which invoices were chased, when, or with what result.
  5. 05Cash flow suffers even though the work is done and the customer is willing to pay.

None of this is a staffing problem. It is a workflow that was never designed to scale, and that is what we fix.

02/How it works

How it works
  1. 01

    Connect your receivables

    We connect to your invoicing or accounting system, so the automation always knows what is outstanding, overdue or promised.

  2. 02

    Design the cadence

    Together we set tone and pacing: a friendly nudge, a firm reminder, then escalation. Different customers and amounts can follow different paths.

  3. 03

    Automate across channels

    Reminders go out by email, two-way SMS or a natural AI voice call, written for the customer and the invoice instead of one blast for everyone.

  4. 04

    Escalate only what matters

    Disputes, hardship cases and silence past your threshold go to your team with full context. The rest resolves without them.

Frequently asked questions
Won't automated collections annoy my customers?

Done crudely, yes. That is why we set tone and pacing with you before anything goes out. A polite, well-spaced, personal reminder is far less irritating than a tense phone call after ninety days of silence, and customers respond to it.

Which channels can it use?

Email, two-way SMS and natural AI voice calls, in whatever mix suits your customers. Messages adapt to the customer and the invoice instead of following one script.

When does a person get involved?

Whenever you decide: disputes, promised payments that lapse, sensitive accounts or balances past a threshold you set. Your team gets the full history, so the conversation starts informed.

Does it work with our accounting software?

We connect to the invoicing and accounting tools you already run, so balances stay in sync and a payment stops the reminders automatically.
