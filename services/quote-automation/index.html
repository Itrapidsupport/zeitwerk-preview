01/The problem

The first good quote usually wins.

The first good quote usually wins.

If a request sits in your inbox for three days while a competitor replies within the hour, the job is gone before you open the pricing spreadsheet.

Where quoting breaks down

  1. 01Pricing lives in one person's head or in a spreadsheet nobody else trusts.
  2. 02Requests arrive by email, phone and web form, and some fall through.
  3. 03Two staff quote the same job two different ways.
  4. 04Sent quotes get followed up when someone remembers, so warm leads expire.
  5. 05Busy season creates a quoting backlog exactly when you can least afford one.

None of this is a staffing problem. It is a workflow that was never designed to scale, and that is what we fix.

02/How it works

How it works
  1. 01

    Capture your pricing logic

    Rates, materials, margins, minimums and the exceptions. We write down how you actually price work and turn it into explicit rules.

  2. 02

    Build the drafting engine

    The system reads each request from email, web form or call notes, pulls out the details that matter, applies your rules and assembles a complete quote.

  3. 03

    Add review and approval

    You set the guardrails. Routine quotes can send automatically or wait for one-click approval, and anything unusual is clearly flagged.

  4. 04

    Automate the follow-up

    Sent quotes get polite, scheduled follow-ups until they are accepted, declined or expired.

05/Questions

Frequently asked questions
How does it keep quotes accurate?

It only applies pricing rules you have approved. Anything outside normal parameters, such as unusual scope, missing information or non-standard pricing, goes to a person for review instead of being sent blind.

Our jobs are custom. Can this still work?

Yes, and that is the usual case. The system drafts the structured majority of each quote and flags the judgment calls for you, which turns a process of days into minutes of review.

Does it connect to our CRM or accounting software?

We build against the CRM, accounting and email tools you already use, so accepted quotes flow into your existing pipeline instead of another disconnected app.

What does quoting automation cost?

It depends on how complex your pricing is and what we connect to. We scope every project individually. Book a free consultation and we will map your quoting workflow and give you a clear recommendation first.
