01/The problem

The report is out of date before the meeting.

The report is out of date before the meeting.

Someone opens six spreadsheets, exports from four systems and spends Friday afternoon building a report that is stale by Monday. Each copy-paste error makes people trust the numbers a little less.

Signs your reporting is running you

  1. 01Reports take hours of manual export, copy and paste every week or month.
  2. 02By the time leadership sees the numbers, they are days old.
  3. 03Each department keeps its own spreadsheet and its own version of the truth.
  4. 04One person knows how the report is built, and it stops when they are away.
  5. 05Errors surface in meetings, and the discussion turns to the data instead of the decision.

None of this is a staffing problem. It is a workflow that was never designed to scale, and that is what we fix.

05/Questions

Asked before every project.

Frequently asked questions
What data sources can you connect?

Most modern business systems: CRMs, accounting platforms, operations software, databases, and the spreadsheets and email exports you rely on today. If the data exists somewhere, we can usually automate its path into the report.

Do we need a data warehouse?

Not necessarily. We size the architecture to the problem, and many businesses get live, trustworthy reporting without heavy infrastructure. If your volume calls for more, we will tell you and design for it.

Can we still get reports by email or PDF?

Yes. The same pipeline can feed a live dashboard and email a formatted PDF pack on whatever schedule you set.

What does reporting automation cost?

It depends on how many sources we connect and how complex the reporting is. Book a free consultation and we will audit your current reporting and show you what to automate first.
