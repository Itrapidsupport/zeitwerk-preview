01/The problem

Shared inboxes fail quietly.

Shared inboxes fail quietly.

A few hundred messages a day and nothing looks broken. The urgent request is just buried under newsletters, the customer has followed up twice, and your best people spend the first hour of every morning deciding what to read.

How high-volume inboxes fail

  1. 01Urgent messages sit unseen behind routine ones for hours.
  2. 02Nobody is sure who owns a message, so everyone answers or no one does.
  3. 03Your most experienced people spend mornings sorting instead of solving.
  4. 04The same questions get hand-typed answers dozens of times a week.
  5. 05Follow-ups slip because nothing tracks what is still waiting on a reply.

None of this is a staffing problem. It is a workflow that was never designed to scale, and that is what we fix.

02/How it works

How it works
  1. 01

    Learn your mail

    We study what you actually receive and define the categories, priorities and owners that match how your team works.

  2. 02

    Classify and route

    Each message is categorized, prioritized and assigned as it arrives. Urgent items are flagged the moment they land.

  3. 03

    Draft the replies

    For routine mail the system drafts a response in your voice for a person to approve, edit or send. Fully automatic replies run only where you switch them on.

  4. 04

    Track and improve

    Nothing closes until it is handled. We review the misses and keep tuning, so accuracy improves with use.

05/Questions

Asked before every project.

Frequently asked questions
What if it files something incorrectly?

We set confidence thresholds, so messages the system is unsure about go to a person instead of being guessed at. Misfiled items are reviewed and fed back in, and the system gets sharper the longer it runs.

Will it send email without us knowing?

Only if you switch that on for specific, well-defined message types. The default is drafts for review: a person approves everything until you are comfortable expanding it.

Does it work with Outlook and Gmail?

Yes. We build on Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, using your existing mailboxes and permissions instead of moving you to a new tool.

What about confidentiality?

Before anything goes live, we walk you through what the system reads, where the data flows and what is stored. We design within your existing email platform and access policies.
