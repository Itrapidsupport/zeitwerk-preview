A missed call is a lead that dials your competitor next, a tenant whose small problem becomes a large one, or a customer who gives up. When the front desk is stretched, or nobody can staff the phones after hours, the calls still come in. They just go unanswered.

05/Questions

Frequently asked questions
Will callers know they are talking to an AI?

We recommend that it says so. Callers care far more about being helped right away than about who answers. The receptionist handles routine matters completely and hands off cleanly when a person is needed.

What happens when the AI cannot handle a call?

We design the escalation rules with you before launch. It can transfer to a person, take a structured message, send an urgent alert or book a callback. Every call produces a transcript, so nothing is lost.

Does this replace my receptionist?

Usually it protects them. The AI takes after-hours calls, overflow and repeat questions, so your team can focus on the conversations that need a person.

What does an AI receptionist cost in Toronto?

It depends on call volume and the systems we connect to, so we scope each project individually. Start with a free consultation. We will audit your call flow and give you a clear recommendation and quote before you commit.
