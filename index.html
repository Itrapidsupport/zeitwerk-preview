05/Process

Six stages, done in order.

We understand the workflow first, then engineer the automation around it. Never the other way around.

Six stages, done in order.
  1. 01

    Discovery and audit

    We study how work moves through your business today and find the bottlenecks worth automating first.
  2. 02

    Solution design

    We architect a system that fits your business, your tools and the scale you expect.
  3. 03

    System build

    We write the automation and integrations, with error handling for the cases that go wrong.
  4. 04

    Integration

    We connect the tools you already run, including your CRM, ERP and email, so they work as one system.
  5. 05

    Testing and QA

    We stress-test the edge cases before launch so the system runs without someone watching it.
  6. 06

    Launch and scale

    We deploy, monitor and keep tuning as your volume and your business change.
